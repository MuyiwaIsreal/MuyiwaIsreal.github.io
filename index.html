GatewayGuard: Payment Gateway Benchmarking

  • Python
  • Chi-Square
  • Logistic Regression
  • scikit-learn
  • statsmodels

Statistical study of 30,000 transactions across Adyen, Flutterwave, PayPal, Razorpay and Stripe to test whether payment failures are driven by the gateway or by amount and geography. A Chi-Square test showed no significant gateway effect, and a logistic regression quantified success odds by gateway, amount and country, supporting evidence-based routing decisions.
