1Problem

  • Each department exported raw ERP data to Excel and built its own reports.
  • Teams reported conflicting numbers from the same data.
  • BI queries against large ERP tables were slow and loaded production.
  • Leadership had no daily, cross-department view.

2Approach

  • Built five Mage pipelines (finance, HR, inventory, CRM, ops KPIs), with the IT/dev team, that aggregate ERPNext into summary tables.
  • Modelled a star schema with shared date, department and account dimensions.
  • Used Power BI Import mode on the small summary tables, with a scheduled daily refresh.

3Result

  • One agreed set of numbers across finance, operations and leadership.
  • Dashboards no longer query the production ERP, and refreshes run automatically.
  • Finance tracks tenant inflows, vendor disbursements and cash position daily.

Statistical analysis · Payments

GatewayGuard: do payment gateways really differ?

PythonpandasSciPystatsmodelsscikit-learn
View code & results

1Problem

Payment failures cost revenue and trust. Is one of five gateways (Adyen, Flutterwave, PayPal, Razorpay, Stripe) actually worse, or are failures driven by amount and geography?

2Approach

Defined success as completed and authorised, ran a Chi-Square test of independence across gateways, then fitted a logistic regression on gateway, amount and country to get odds ratios.

3Result

No significant gateway or amount effect (χ² = 0.67, p = 0.95, Cramér's V = 0.005). The one real signal: transactions from India had ~17% lower odds of success (OR 0.83, p = 0.006). Recommendation: route on fees, not perceived reliability, and investigate the India corridor.

ⓘSynthetic data. The 30,000 transactions are simulated to mirror real gateway logs; no real customer or company data is used.

Daily cash-flow and lead-engagement datasets

1Problem

Finance reconciled disbursements and collections from separate sources, and sales couldn't see all communication with a lead when some of it happened on support tickets.

2Approach

Wrote one query that unions daily disbursements and collections into a single dataset with a USD-normalised amount, and a multi-step CTE query that joins each lead's direct and ticket-linked communications.

3Result

Two analytics-ready datasets feeding the cash-flow, reconciliation and CRM engagement dashboards, replacing manual exports.
